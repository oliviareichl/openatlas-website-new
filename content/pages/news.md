**First (early) version of OpenAtlas-Discovery released**  

The first version of [OpenAtlas-Discovery](https://github.com/craws/OpenAtlas-Discovery) is released. It is a general presentation site for data of OpenAtlas projects and can also be used as starting point for more specialised presentation sites. A preview with demo data is available here: [Demo](https://frontend-demo-dev.openatlas.eu/)
